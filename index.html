Production Logic

Solving the 72 DPI limitation

Browser-based design tools traditionally struggle with print resolution because web canvases export at 72 pixels per inch (the standard for screens). When these files are sent to professional printing RIP software, they are often scaled incorrectly, resulting in "jagged" edges on fabric.

LithoPrint Studio bypasses this limitation. Our export engine performs a pixel-by-pixel metadata rewrite of the PNG binary. We manually inject a physical dimension chunk (pHYs) that forces professional software like Photoshop or Illustrator to recognize the file at 300 DPI immediately. This ensures your grain and ink density settings translate perfectly from the screen to the shirt.

Industry Standards

The Physics of the Ink Halo

Transparent PNGs use anti-aliasing to smooth out edges. This process creates a "fringe" of semi-transparent grey pixels at the boundary of your graphic. While this looks smooth on a white digital background, it is a disaster for black t-shirt printing.

Printers interpret these semi-transparent pixels as light colors and attempt to print a white underbase beneath them. This creates a visible "white halo" around your design. Our tool fixes this by separating color from opacity. We override every pixel to your target ink color while using the "Black Crush" engine to cauterize the edges—ensuring you have either 100% ink or 100% transparency.

Technical FAQ

Exit to Studio

Does this tool downsample my images?

No. All processing happens at the original resolution of your file. If you upload a 4000px asset, you export a 4000px production file. We preserve every pixel for professional DTG and screen printing.

What is the "Black Crush" setting?

Black Crush is the opacity threshold. It takes grey or semi-transparent pixels and forces them to become 100% solid ink. Use this if your graphics look "faint" or "dusty" on a white shirt preview.

How do I use the Crop Mode?

Click "Crop Mode", then draw a box directly on your graphic. Once the red box matches your desired area, click the "Apply Crop" button that appears in the sidebar.

Can I process text without an image?

Yes. If no image is imported, the tool generates a high-resolution 3000px white slate. You can type your text, apply grain and black crush, and download it as a standalone transparent stencil.
