Why does my PNG still have a white outline?

Standard removers leave anti-aliased grey pixels. LithoPrint Pro uses the "Black Crush" engine to force those pixels into transparency or solid ink.

Is this tool safe for 300 DPI files?

Yes. The tool processes the original pixel dimensions of your source file. We do not downsample. Your 300 DPI master stays 300 DPI.
