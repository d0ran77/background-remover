Type Theory 01

The Psychology of the Stencil

Typography is the skeleton of apparel design. A simple phrase like "PRODUCE" takes on different weights depending on its container. v4.8 allows you to arc and wave standard production fonts, mimicking the physical distortion found in low-quality photocopy stencils or warped screen meshes.

Industry 02

DPI vs. Physical Scale

Standard canvas tools export at 72 DPI, which represents screen density. LithoPrint Pro manually rewrites the metadata of your PNG so it opens in professional software at 300 DPI. This ensures that a 3000px design prints at exactly 10 inches wide at full resolution.

How do I Curve text?

Select "Arc Curve" from the line style dropdown next to the font selection. Use the "Effect Strength" slider to adjust the bend. Positive numbers arc the text upwards; negative numbers arc it downwards.

Can I use specific Pantone Hex codes?

Yes. Use the text box inside the "Production Ink Color" block to paste any 6-digit hex code. This ensures your stencil exports match your target ink batch exactly.
