Technical 01

Understanding PNG Metadata (The 300 DPI Rule)

When you export a graphic from a standard web browser, it defaults to 72 DPI. For professional apparel printing, this is often too low for automated RIP software. LithoPrint Studio v4.1 introduces a custom metadata injection layer. We take your processed pixel array and manually rewrite the physical dimension headers (the pHYs chunk) to force a 300 DPI reading.

This means your file will open in Photoshop or Illustrator at the correct physical print size (e.g., 10 inches wide) rather than appearing massive. It ensures your Black Crush and Grain settings translate perfectly to the fabric without the need for manual resizing.

Entry 02

The Death of the White Halo

LithoPrint Pro fixes the "white halo" issue by separating luminance from alpha. We override pixel colors to your target ink while "crushing" the density, ensuring printers only see true ink or transparency. No anti-aliasing fuzz.

Technical FAQ

Exit to Studio

Does this export 300 DPI?

Yes. We manually inject the 300 DPI metadata into every PNG export.

Standard canvas exports are 72 DPI. LithoPrint v4.1 rewrites the image headers so professional printing software recognizes the high-resolution density immediately.

What is "Black Crush"?

It is the threshold where grey pixels are forced to become solid ink. If your text looks grey on a white shirt, increase the Black Crush to ensure 100% ink density.
