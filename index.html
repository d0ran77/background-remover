Masterclass 01

The 300 DPI Rule & Binary Header Injection

In the apparel industry, DPI (Dots Per Inch) is critical. Standard web exports are 72 DPI. We manually rewrite the pHYs chunk of the PNG binary to ensure professional RIP software recognizes the file at native 300 DPI upon import.

Technical 02

Eliminating White Fringe in DTG Printing

Transparent PNGs often have anti-aliased grey edges. Printers see these as "light colors" and print a white underbase, creating a halo. Our Black Crush technology maps luminance to absolute opacity, forcing a clean 100% ink or 100% transparency result.

How do I use the new Line Envelopes?

Select a style like "Flag" or "Bulge" from the line style dropdown. Adjust the Strength slider to warp the text. Flag creates a wind-like ripple, while Bulge simulates a 3D lens effect. v5.2 ensures that your characters remain upright and readable even at extreme strengths.

Does this tool downsample my images?

No. We perform 1:1 pixel mapping. If you upload a 5000px asset, you export a 5000px asset at 300 DPI.
